# Invoice, Receipt, and Document-Understanding Datasets

## Which datasets are useful, what do they contain, and how are they accessed?

### Takeaway
CORD and SROIE are the most directly relevant public benchmarks for receipt parsing; DocILE is the strongest listed option for business-document/invoice KIE and line-item extraction. FUNSD/XFUND are form-understanding transfer datasets, Kleister covers long-form PDF field extraction, and RVL-CDIP/IIT-CDIP are primarily document-image classification/pretraining corpora rather than invoice extraction benchmarks. Public availability does not automatically mean the underlying document imagery is cleared for unrestricted commercial reuse.

### Cited Findings

#### Dataset-by-dataset survey

| **IIT-CDIP (often called IIT-CDIP Test Collection)** | Approximately 11 million scanned document images/pages from the legacy Enron-era collection; predominantly English and heterogeneous correspondence, forms and business/legal records. | Large-scale image/text collection for document classification/retrieval and pretraining; not an invoice/receipt structured extraction corpus. | Distributed through the University of Illinois/IIT-CDIP collection and related research access channels; access and use terms are more constrained than a permissive open benchmark. Some versions expose train/test evaluation subsets; there is no single universally comparable field-extraction split. [IIT-CDIP project](https://ir.cs.georgetown.edu/ir_course/iitcdip/) | **Very low direct fit**; useful only for scale-oriented pretraining/classification. Large, noisy, legacy scans and provenance/privacy concerns require careful review. |

**Field coverage interpretation:** CORD is unusually rich on receipt item rows and payment totals; SROIE gives only a few receipt header targets in its extraction task. DocILE best addresses invoice/business-document fields and line-item localization. Image-class datasets should not be mistaken for extraction datasets merely because their classes include “invoice” or “receipt.” [CORD](https://github.com/clovaai/cord); [SROIE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.10213); [DocILE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.05658); [RVL-CDIP](https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.07058)

### Inferences
- For supervised receipt extraction, start with CORD plus SROIE, keeping their distinct ontologies and source domains visible rather than merging labels blindly.
- For invoice KIE/line items, DocILE is the most aligned listed benchmark; EPHOIE can add Chinese invoice coverage if its research-use terms and downloadable version are confirmed.
- FUNSD and XFUND are most valuable as layout/entity-linking transfer evaluations, not as substitutes for labeled transactional documents.
- RVL-CDIP/IIT-CDIP may support representation pretraining or document categorization but cannot directly supervise field extraction without additional annotation.

### Gaps
- EPHOIE's primary paper/project URL and exact license/access controls could not be independently verified in the available source fetches; numbers/split above reflect commonly cited descriptions and should be checked against the authors' released paper/data before operational use.
- XFUND's split counts and license may vary by release; inspect the versioned official repository/data card before comparing scores.
- Several dataset owners distinguish code license from data/image license. The paper or repository alone may not establish clearance for commercial training, redistribution, or derived datasets.

## What are the practical risks and how should suitability be assessed?

### Takeaway
Dataset size alone is a poor proxy for deployment relevance: language, capture channel, business geography, document template overlap, annotation ontology and field completeness matter more. Benchmark test sets are often small or intentionally hidden, and source-document privacy/copyright and split leakage should be treated as first-order evaluation risks.

### Cited Findings
- SROIE's challenge offers 1,000 whole receipts with 600 trainval and 400 test; the organizer report frames separate localization, OCR and key-information extraction tasks, so a strong KIE result does not establish end-to-end OCR performance. [SROIE challenge paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.10213); [dataset mirror task description](https://github.com/zzzDavid/ICDAR-2019-SROIE)
- CORD's release is 1,000 labeled examples split 800/100/100, while the project describes more than 11,000 receipts collected; it also documents legal removal of several initially proposed fields. This means collection scale, public annotation scale and label coverage are not interchangeable. [CORD repository](https://github.com/clovaai/cord)
- DocILE's labeled set is 6.7k documents, with separate synthetic and unlabeled pools, and deliberately includes zero-/few-shot and familiar-layout test cases. [DocILE paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.05658)
- Kleister's split documentation discusses hash-based separation and explicitly notes that splitting by company can avoid company overlap across train/test; its 12GB PDF originals are retrieved separately through git-annex. [Kleister Charity repository](https://github.com/applicaai/kleister-charity)
- RVL-CDIP is a 16-way image-classification benchmark. Its label set includes invoice/receipt categories, but the published benchmark task does not provide field-level invoice/receipt extraction labels. [RVL-CDIP paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.07058)
- CORD declares CC BY 4.0 for its project data; by contrast, a repository's code license (for example the SROIE solution mirror's MIT license) should not be construed as a license for source images. [CORD license/repository](https://github.com/clovaai/cord); [SROIE mirror](https://github.com/zzzDavid/ICDAR-2019-SROIE)

### Inferences
- **Geographic and language domain shift:** Indonesian food receipts (CORD), Malaysian/English receipts (SROIE), and Chinese e-invoices (EPHOIE) do not span all receipt/invoice formats, scripts, tax conventions, currencies, or capture conditions.
- **Template/entity leakage:** random page splits can place near-duplicate vendors, repeated invoice templates, or documents from the same organization in both training and test sets. Group by vendor/issuer/time/source where possible; report both in-domain and unseen-vendor results.
- **OCR and annotation ceiling:** evaluate OCR, field localization, field value normalization, and end-to-end extraction separately. A parsed text benchmark can conceal OCR failure; weak or inconsistent ground truth can make exact-match metrics misleading.
- **Privacy/provenance:** public access is not proof that personal, financial, or third-party copyright material can be republished or used commercially. Conduct review of licenses, terms, personal data, and source acquisition for each dataset/version.
- **Benchmark-size uncertainty:** small held-out sets produce noisy estimates, particularly after slicing by language, field, vendor or image quality; include confidence intervals and a private, temporally separated evaluation set.

### Gaps
- Exact terms of use and current availability can change, particularly for challenge portals, XFUND, EPHOIE, DocILE and IIT-CDIP; a pre-deployment legal and data-card review remains necessary.
- The listed public sources do not provide a consistent, apples-to-apples estimate of duplicate vendors/templates, demographic or privacy risk, or field-level annotation error across all datasets.

## Which primary papers and repositories should be consulted first?

### Takeaway
Use dataset maintainers' pages/repos for release structure and current downloads, paired with the original benchmark papers for task definitions and evaluation. This is especially important where code and data have separate licenses or where multiple dataset versions exist.

### Cited Findings
- CORD release details, class hierarchy, version history and CC BY declaration: [clovaai/cord](https://github.com/clovaai/cord); original workshop citation is listed in that project README.
- SROIE task definitions and competition design: [Huang et al., ICDAR2019 Competition on Scanned Receipt OCR and Information Extraction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.10213); [ICDAR RRC challenge page](https://rrc.cvc.uab.es/?ch=13&com=introduction).
- FUNSD dataset/task definition: [Jaume et al., FUNSD: A Dataset for Form Understanding in Noisy Scanned Documents](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.13538); [project data page](https://guillaumejaume.github.io/FUNSD/).
- XFUND and multilingual document understanding: [LayoutXLM paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.08836); [Microsoft UniLM LayoutXLM source](https://github.com/microsoft/unilm/tree/master/layoutxlm).
- DocILE benchmark and splits: [Šimsa et al., DocILE Benchmark for Document Information Localization and Extraction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.05658); [rossumai/docile](https://github.com/rossumai/docile).
- Kleister Charity task and dataset: [applicaai/kleister-charity](https://github.com/applicaai/kleister-charity); original benchmark paper linked by repository as [Kleister: A benchmark for information extraction from documents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.02356).
- RVL-CDIP: [Harley et al., Evaluation of Deep Convolutional Nets for Document Image Classification and Retrieval](https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.07058); [author dataset page](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~aharley/rvl-cdip/).
- IIT-CDIP collection: [project overview/access information](https://ir.cs.georgetown.edu/ir_course/iitcdip/).
- EPHOIE: published benchmark is commonly cited as “A Dataset for Chinese Invoice Understanding” / EPHOIE; primary publication and official data hosting could not be reliably identified in this research pass. Search via the dataset name and validate bibliographic record against the actual release before citing.

### Inferences
- Maintain a dataset manifest with citation, URL, version/hash, access date, license/terms, language, unit (page/document/receipt), split, fields, and provenance; this makes results reproducible even when download portals change.
- The benchmark list mixes tasks and units: document pages (RVL-CDIP/IIT-CDIP), forms (FUNSD/XFUND), receipt images (CORD/SROIE), invoices/business documents (DocILE/EPHOIE), and multipage reports (Kleister). Compare only after clearly stating task and unit.

### Gaps
- The exact primary bibliographic record for EPHOIE and official IIT-CDIP terms are not established by the links available above; confirm from original proceedings/data portal before final publication.
