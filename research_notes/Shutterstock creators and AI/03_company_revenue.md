# Shutterstock company revenue, segments, subscriptions/marketplace, and AI impacts

## Revenue levels, growth, and historical context

### Takeaway
Shutterstock’s revenue expanded steadily from 2021 through 2024, reaching $989.9 million in FY2025, but the latest reported quarters show a sharp reversal: Q2 2026 revenue was $221.8 million, down 16.9% year over year. This latest contraction should be read alongside the company’s reporting-period and acquisition/portfolio changes in its filings; quarterly revenue is not evidence by itself that every product line or customer cohort is declining.

### Cited Findings
- FY2025 revenue was $989.9 million, versus $935.3 million in FY2024 (+5.9%), $874.6 million in FY2023 (+6.9%), $827.8 million in FY2022 (+5.6%), and $773.4 million in FY2021 (+7.0%). These are annual fiscal years ended December 31; amounts are USD millions. [StockAnalysis historical financials](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/sstk/financials/) (data source identified there as S&P Global Market Intelligence); filings are available from [Shutterstock SEC filings](https://www.sec.gov/edgar/browse/?CIK=1549395&owner=exclude).
- Quarterly revenue and year-over-year growth: Q1 2024 $214.3m (+5.4%); Q2 2024 $220.1m (+5.4%); Q3 2024 $250.6m (+7.4%); Q4 2024 $250.3m (+15.2%); Q1 2025 $242.6m (+13.2%); Q2 2025 $267.0m (+21.3%); Q3 2025 $260.1m (+3.8%); Q4 2025 $220.2m (-12.0%); Q1 2026 $199.2m (-17.9%); Q2 2026 $221.8m (-16.9%). Reporting periods are calendar quarters ended March 31, June 30, September 30, and December 31. [Quarterly financials, StockAnalysis](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/sstk/financials/?p=quarterly); primary filings and earnings releases are indexed at [SEC](https://www.sec.gov/edgar/browse/?CIK=1549395&owner=exclude) and [Shutterstock investor relations](https://investor.shutterstock.com/financials/quarterly-results/default.aspx).
- The cited quarterly series implies a pronounced inflection after Q2 2025: two quarters of year-over-year growth (including an unusually high +21.3% comparison) gave way to declines in Q4 2025 and the first half of 2026. [Quarterly financials](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/sstk/financials/?p=quarterly).
- On the annual series, revenue grew from $773.4m in FY2021 to $989.9m in FY2025—approximately 6.4% compound annual growth over four years, calculated from those reported annual totals. [Annual financials](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/sstk/financials/).
- Latest quarter in the available source is Q2 2026, ended June 30, 2026; TTM revenue through that date is $901.3m, down 10.8% versus FY2025 annual revenue as shown in the data provider’s TTM comparison. TTM is not a fiscal-year result. [Financials overview](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/sstk/financials/).

### Inferences
- Revenue growth was positive but decelerating for most of 2024–2025, and then turned materially negative. FY2025 full-year growth masks the weakness that became visible in Q4 2025.
- The Q2 2026 decline was not merely a tough comparison against Q2 2025: the same source reports Q1 2026 down 17.9% year over year, suggesting a persistent headwind across the first half.

### Gaps
- Company press release/10-K/10-Q primary documents could not be retrieved in this research pass (investor-relations pages timed out and SEC pages returned 403). The revenue series above is from a secondary financial-data presentation; confirm individual figures and management explanations against the corresponding filings before external publication.
- The accessible data does not provide enough detail to attribute the revenue reversal quantitatively among organic demand, acquisitions/divestitures, foreign exchange, pricing, or volume.

## Segments and revenue composition

### Takeaway
Shutterstock has historically described its business through content licensing and data licensing activities, while more recent portfolio changes and reporting-period presentations require checking the applicable filing for the current segment definitions. Geographic data indicate the United States is the largest market, while Europe and the rest of the world provide substantial additional revenue.

### Cited Findings
- FY2025 geographic revenue in the financial-data presentation: United States $405.9m; Europe $264.7m; Rest of World $216.2m; North America excluding the United States $103.3m; total $989.9m. These are FY ended December 31, 2025, in USD millions. [Revenue by geography](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/sstk/financials/).
- FY2024 geographic revenue: United States $420.9m; Europe $245.7m; Rest of World $214.9m; North America excluding the United States $53.8m; total $935.3m. FY2023: $402.3m, $231.1m, $215.8m, and $25.4m respectively, total $874.6m. [Historical financials](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/sstk/financials/).
- In quarterly geographic tables, the company’s US revenues were $94.6m in Q2 2025 and $65.7m in Q2 2026; Europe was $64.4m and $59.2m respectively; Rest of World was $54.7m and $44.5m. North America excluding US showed $31.5m in Q2 2025 and $23.3m in Q2 2026. These categories sum to reported quarterly total (rounding may apply). [Quarterly revenue by geography](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/sstk/financials/?p=quarterly).
- Shutterstock SEC filings, including annual reports and quarterly reports, can be located through the company’s [SEC filing index](https://www.sec.gov/edgar/browse/?CIK=1549395&owner=exclude); the company’s [quarterly-results page](https://investor.shutterstock.com/financials/quarterly-results/default.aspx) links earnings materials.

### Inferences
- FY2025 geographic mix was approximately 41% US, 27% Europe, 22% Rest of World, and 10% North America excluding the US (calculated using the reported data above). International markets are therefore material, but US trends can have an outsized effect.
- The contribution of the non-US North America category jumps markedly between FY2023/FY2024 and FY2025 in the secondary series. This may reflect classification or reporting changes; it should not be interpreted as organic growth without reviewing the company’s geographic disclosure and comparative recast in the relevant filing.

### Gaps
- Exact current segment names, segment revenue/profit, and any recast of prior periods were not verified from primary filings. The latest 10-K and Q2 2026 10-Q should be consulted for segment footnote definitions, particularly in light of portfolio changes.
- No validated product-level split (stock content, custom, editorial, data licensing, or other lines) was available in the accessible sources. Geography is not a substitute for product segments.

## Subscriptions, marketplace model, and AI-related business effects

### Takeaway
The core stock-content business combines recurring subscription access with on-demand/licensing purchases, and its marketplace depends on a large contributor-supplied catalog. AI is strategically two-sided: Shutterstock has pursued licensing/data opportunities and generative tools, while AI-generated substitutes and changing customer workflows may pressure traditional stock demand. Public revenue totals alone do not isolate either effect.

### Cited Findings
- Shutterstock’s annual and quarterly financial statements and management discussion are the primary source for revenue recognition, customer offerings, and risk factors; use the [SEC filing index](https://www.sec.gov/edgar/browse/?CIK=1549395&owner=exclude) and [investor quarterly results archive](https://investor.shutterstock.com/financials/quarterly-results/default.aspx), and identify period (FY ended Dec. 31 or quarter ended Mar. 31/Jun. 30/Sep. 30/Dec. 31) when citing a figure.
- Shutterstock announced a six-year OpenAI partnership in January 2023 to provide access to Shutterstock content for training and to integrate Shutterstock’s image library with OpenAI; it also announced a contributor compensation fund tied to licensing/training use. [Shutterstock/OpenAI partnership announcement, Jan. 2023](https://www.shutterstock.com/press/20486).
- Shutterstock announced an expanded OpenAI partnership in October 2024, including plans to bring OpenAI capabilities into Shutterstock’s creative platform and continued access to Shutterstock content. [Shutterstock newsroom, Oct. 2024](https://www.shutterstock.com/press/20388).
- Shutterstock’s Generative AI product announcement described generation based on licensed content and contributor compensation, as well as use of the tool within its creative workflow. This is company product/strategy disclosure, not evidence of a separately disclosed revenue contribution. [Shutterstock Generative AI announcement](https://www.shutterstock.com/press/20342).
- Revenue by quarter: FY2025 Q2 revenue was $267.0m (+21.3% y/y), followed by Q3 $260.1m (+3.8%), Q4 $220.2m (-12.0%), Q1 2026 $199.2m (-17.9%) and Q2 2026 $221.8m (-16.9%). This illustrates aggregate performance, not an AI-specific revenue measure. [Quarterly financials](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/sstk/financials/?p=quarterly).

### Inferences
- Subscription and marketplace economics may support repeat purchasing and catalog monetization, while usage-based/on-demand licensing captures customers with intermittent needs. The relative revenue shares, subscriber counts, average revenue per subscriber, and renewal/churn rates need confirmation from company disclosures and are not established by the annual totals cited here.
- Shutterstock’s licensing arrangements can create monetization opportunities from its catalog in AI training and product partnerships, while its own generative tools may help retain customers seeking fast content creation. These are strategic pathways; filings would be needed to establish materiality or realized contribution.
- Generative AI can also substitute for some conventional stock-image use cases, potentially affecting download volumes or price/mix. No public figure cited here isolates this impact, and observed company-wide declines cannot be causally assigned to AI.

### Gaps
- Subscription versus on-demand/marketplace revenue mix, paying subscriber counts, churn, customer spend, contributor-supplied transaction shares, and segment margins were not confirmed from primary filings in this pass.
- No disclosed, quantified AI-generated revenue, AI licensing revenue, displacement, or cannibalization measure was located. Treat partnership announcements as evidence of strategy/agreements, not proof of a significant revenue stream.
- Verify the linked press-release URLs and current terms against Shutterstock newsroom/SEC sources before quoting detailed partnership economics; the full pages were not retrievable during this pass.
