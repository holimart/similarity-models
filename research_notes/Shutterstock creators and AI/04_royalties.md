# Shutterstock Creator Royalties

Research checked September 27, 2026. “Current” below means the public Shutterstock Contributor Earnings Schedule and currently published Contributor help/Terms pages accessed on that date. The payout schedule is dynamic; contributors should check it directly for later changes.

## What are the current royalty levels, rates, thresholds, and reset rules?

### Takeaway

Shutterstock currently has six independently tracked image and video levels, paying 15%–40% of the customer-paid price per licensed asset. Qualifying marketplace licenses accumulate by calendar year and each category resets to Level 1 on January 1; Data and Unlimited licenses do not count toward these level thresholds.

### Cited Findings
- The current Earnings Schedule lists image thresholds/rates: Level 1, up to 100 licenses, 15%; Level 2, 101–250, 20%; Level 3, 251–500, 25%; Level 4, 501–2,500, 30%; Level 5, 2,501–25,000, 35%; Level 6, over 25,000, 40%. The schedule says levels reset each calendar year beginning January 1. [Shutterstock Earnings Schedule](https://submit.shutterstock.com/payouts) (accessed September 27, 2026).
- Video levels are separate: Level 1, up to 10, 15%; Level 2, 11–50, 20%; Level 3, 51–250, 25%; Level 4, 251–5,000, 30%; Level 5, 5,001–25,000, 35%; Level 6, over 25,000, 40%. [Shutterstock Earnings Schedule](https://submit.shutterstock.com/payouts) (accessed September 27, 2026).
- Image and video levels are based on the number of licenses from the Shutterstock marketplace in the current calendar year, excluding Data and Unlimited licenses. [Shutterstock Earnings Schedule](https://submit.shutterstock.com/payouts) (accessed September 27, 2026).
- Shutterstock’s help page confirms there are six levels for images and videos, that the categories progress independently, and that all levels reset to Level 1 on January 1. [What happens when I reach a new earnings level?](https://submit.shutterstock.com/help/en/articles/10594581-what-happens-when-i-reach-a-new-earnings-level) (April 21, 2025).
- Royalties are percentages of the price Shutterstock receives for licensing content; dollar amounts vary with the customer’s plan, license (including Standard vs. Enhanced), and price per asset. Subscription commission uses the per-asset price based on the full subscription allotment, not the customer’s actual usage. [Contributor Earnings Structure FAQ](https://submit.shutterstock.com/help/en/articles/10594653-contributor-earnings-structure-faq) (December 10, 2025); [How much will I earn per download?](https://submit.shutterstock.com/help/en/articles/10594609-how-much-will-i-earn-per-download) (January 27, 2026).
- The current schedule additionally states that an invited Unlimited-program contributor receives royalties under the Subscriber Share model, and that 50% of each Unlimited subscriber’s net revenue is distributed among contributors whose content the subscriber downloaded. [Shutterstock Earnings Schedule](https://submit.shutterstock.com/payouts) (accessed September 27, 2026); [Unlimited Downloads Subscriber Share](https://submit.shutterstock.com/help/en/articles/11487761) (February 12, 2026).

### Inferences
- The threshold units are annual category-specific license counts, not lifetime earnings or portfolio size; therefore a contributor can be at different levels for images and video and must re-earn levels after each New Year reset.
- “Up to 40%” is a percentage of Shutterstock’s price received for the asset/license, not a guarantee of a fixed dollar amount or 40% of retail subscription sticker price.

### Gaps
- The public schedule does not specify how partial/adjusted transactions affect license counts beyond explicitly excluding Data and Unlimited licenses. For edge cases, account-level earnings records or Contributor Support would be needed.

## How are different licensing types paid, and what are payout minimums and timing?

### Takeaway

Standard marketplace downloads pay a level-dependent share of the customer price. Separate programs use other formulas: Data Licensing shares a pooled Contributor Fund, while invited Unlimited contributors split a subscriber-specific share. Payments are monthly and automatic, subject to the contributor’s minimum, payment setup, tax approval, and eligibility.

### Cited Findings
- The Contributor Terms say Shutterstock pays a royalty for each unique download for which it receives payment; the current Earnings Schedule supplies the rates. Duplicate downloads of the same item by one customer are paid once. Payments/statements are issued monthly, around the 15th for the previous month. [Contributor Terms of Service, §8(a)–(b)](https://submit.shutterstock.com/legal/terms?language=en) (page states effective September 5, 2023; accessed September 27, 2026).
- The current Terms set a minimum payout of US$25 per accounting period. Earnings below the minimum, or earnings without a valid electronic payment account, roll into the next accounting period. The contributor payment settings determine payment minimum and method; the official payment FAQ lists Payoneer, PayPal, and Skrill and says payout is automatic. [Contributor Terms of Service, §8(c)](https://submit.shutterstock.com/legal/terms?language=en) (effective September 5, 2023); [How do I get paid for my work?](https://submit.shutterstock.com/help/en/articles/10617435-how-do-i-get-paid-for-my-work) (July 17, 2025).
- Payment calculation starts after the new month begins; payment processing via Payoneer, PayPal, or Skrill is usually between the 7th and 15th. A current approved tax form is required, and some countries have a 90-day waiting period. Minimums can be configured in Account Settings; unpaid accrued earnings roll forward if the chosen minimum is not met. [How do I get paid for my work?](https://submit.shutterstock.com/help/en/articles/10617435-how-do-i-get-paid-for-my-work) (July 17, 2025).
- Shutterstock’s Data Licensing Contributor Fund pays contributors from a pooled fund as it accumulates revenue for distribution; an individual share is proportionate to the volume of that contributor’s content and metadata in purchased datasets. Shutterstock describes the average corporate royalty rate as 20% of revenue received for data licenses. Data earnings may appear in a separate Contributor Fund column rather than as item downloads. [Shutterstock Data Licensing and the Contributor Fund](https://submit.shutterstock.com/help/en/articles/10594694-shutterstock-data-licensing-and-the-contributor-fund) (updated within the week of retrieval; page also describes launch and opt-out chronology).
- Data Licensing is optional through account settings; Shutterstock says separate image and video data licensing controls were introduced in August 2024. [Shutterstock Data Licensing and the Contributor Fund](https://submit.shutterstock.com/help/en/articles/10594694-shutterstock-data-licensing-and-the-contributor-fund) (updated within the week of retrieval).
- Unlimited downloads are initially limited to contributors invited to a pilot. For those included, 50% of net subscription revenue from each subscriber is allocated among that subscriber’s downloaded-content contributors using item-point weighting; net revenue deducts specified costs and adjustments. Unlimited revenue participates in the ordinary monthly payment cycle and does not count toward standard earnings levels. [Unlimited Downloads Subscription Contributor FAQ](https://submit.shutterstock.com/help/en/articles/11498880-unlimited-downloads-subscription-contributor-faq) (July 18, 2025); [Unlimited Downloads Subscriber Share](https://submit.shutterstock.com/help/en/articles/11487761) (February 12, 2026); [Shutterstock Earnings Schedule](https://submit.shutterstock.com/payouts) (accessed September 27, 2026).
- The Terms note that percentage-based royalties are calculated on the sales price actually received after applicable refunds, cancellations, overpayments, taxes, duties, currency costs, and similar deductions; royalties may also be adjusted for refunds/chargebacks. [Contributor Terms of Service, §8(f)–(h)](https://submit.shutterstock.com/legal/terms?language=en) (effective September 5, 2023).

### Inferences
- Do not compare a royalty percentage directly to advertised customer subscription prices without knowing Shutterstock’s per-asset price calculation and the plan/license used.
- Data Fund and Unlimited earnings should not be assumed to equal or use the same per-download rate as ordinary marketplace image/video licenses.

### Gaps
- Data Fund payout timing is described only as periodic when the fund accumulates significant revenue; no fixed distribution schedule or individual calculation inputs are published.
- Payable earnings may be affected by tax withholding and country/payment-provider requirements; exact net payout depends on the contributor’s status and account settings.

## What changed recently, and how does current policy differ from historical policy?

### Takeaway

The foundational change was June 1, 2020: Shutterstock replaced its prior lifetime-earnings/fixed-rate elements with annual download-based levels and percentage-of-license-price commissions. Current public documentation retains the annual reset system, while newer separate compensation streams cover Data Licensing and invited Unlimited subscriptions.

### Cited Findings
- Shutterstock’s Contributor Earnings Structure FAQ says the new structure began June 1, 2020, moved all licensing plans to a percentage-based model, and replaced a prior structure based on lifetime earnings and (for some plans) fixed rates. The FAQ says the new structure added two levels and offered up to 40%. [Contributor Earnings Structure FAQ](https://submit.shutterstock.com/help/en/articles/10594653-contributor-earnings-structure-faq) (December 10, 2025; describing the 2020 change).
- The same FAQ says levels reset annually to reward work currently performing well, rather than tenure/lifetime performance; it acknowledges that the new structure could pay less in some cases than the previous model. [Contributor Earnings Structure FAQ](https://submit.shutterstock.com/help/en/articles/10594653-contributor-earnings-structure-faq) (December 10, 2025).
- The current Earnings Schedule retains six annual levels and separate image/video thresholds, up to 40%, and clarifies Data and Unlimited licenses are excluded from level-count calculations. [Shutterstock Earnings Schedule](https://submit.shutterstock.com/payouts) (accessed September 27, 2026).
- Shutterstock says it launched data licensing/datasets in July 2021; an opt-out control for future datasets was added in January 2023; and separate image/video data licensing toggles were introduced in August 2024. Dataset earnings are handled through the Contributor Fund, rather than ordinary creative-marketplace downloads. [Shutterstock Data Licensing and the Contributor Fund](https://submit.shutterstock.com/help/en/articles/10594694-shutterstock-data-licensing-and-the-contributor-fund) (updated within the week of retrieval).
- Shutterstock launched an Unlimited downloads subscription pilot for a small group of invited contributors; the July 2025 contributor FAQ documents invitation-based participation and the Subscriber Share earnings approach. The Subscriber Share detail page, dated February 12, 2026, defines the split as 50% of subscriber net revenue distributed using item-point weights. [Unlimited Downloads Subscription Contributor FAQ](https://submit.shutterstock.com/help/en/articles/11498880-unlimited-downloads-subscription-contributor-faq) (July 18, 2025); [Unlimited Downloads Subscriber Share](https://submit.shutterstock.com/help/en/articles/11487761) (February 12, 2026).
- Current per-download guidance, updated January 27, 2026, emphasizes that plan, license, and level all affect payment, and describes different reporting categories for subscriptions, on-demand, enhanced licenses, API/custom sales, and footage. [How much will I earn per download?](https://submit.shutterstock.com/help/en/articles/10594609-how-much-will-i-earn-per-download) (January 27, 2026).

### Inferences
- Historical descriptions of fixed cents-per-download rates or lifetime earnings levels should not be presented as current standard marketplace policy. Some downloads may still show small fixed amounts (for example, unusual or legacy/custom products), but the current general rule is percentage-based and customer-plan dependent.
- Recent addition of Data and Unlimited programs means “Shutterstock royalty rate” is not one universal figure: applicable calculation depends on whether the license is ordinary marketplace, Data Fund, or Unlimited Subscriber Share.

### Gaps
- Shutterstock’s public FAQ summarizes the pre-June 2020 model but does not provide a complete historical schedule of all legacy fixed rates or lifetime thresholds. Exact historical values should not be repeated without an archived official schedule.
- No official public change log was located that lists every revision to current image/video thresholds. The live Earnings Schedule is the best source for present-day tiers; its page does not show an effective date or revision history.
